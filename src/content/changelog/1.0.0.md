- **Timeline editing:** trim, split, reorder and replace clips, with snapping to edges, beats and the playhead. Speed from 0.1x to 100x with curves, reverse, freeze frames, undo, and drafts saved as you go.
- **AutoCut and templates:** cut clips to the beat of a song, start from one of 12 templates, or turn any project into a template.
- **Looks:** filters plus your own .cube LUTs, 46 effects, 30 transitions, and keyframes with easing for movement, opacity, masks and colour.
- **Text and stickers:** 24 fonts or your own, outline, glow, curved text and animations, 35 text templates, text to speech, emoji, animated and 3D stickers, GIFs and drawing.
- **Layers and sound:** picture-in-picture with blend modes, masks and chroma key; several music tracks; voiceover with voice effects; audio taken from any video.
- **Export:** 480p to 4K at 24 to 60 fps, H.264 or HEVC, HDR where the phone supports it, in the background. Also GIF, audio only and single frames. Never a watermark.
- **Cloud AI check (optional):** Settings → Cloud AI → Check cloud AI tries speech to text on a phrase your phone speaks. Only the sound is sent, after you agree.
