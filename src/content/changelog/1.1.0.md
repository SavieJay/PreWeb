- **Automatic captions:** in eight languages, or Auto to let it tell, and six styles, including one that lights up the word being spoken. Fix any word or timing in the Captions panel, hear a line again, or save the captions as an .srt file. Only the sound is sent, after you agree, and music is left out.
- **AI Edit:** describe the video you want and Claude proposes the changes. You see every change before it's kept: switch off what you don't want, refine, or apply. It reads a written summary of your project, never the video.
- **AI on your phone:** remove the background behind people, auto reframe for any shape, reduce background noise, and make text and stickers follow a person. All free, and nothing is sent.
- **Tools with free tries every month:** edit by transcript (remove filler words and pauses), long video to shorts, export several shapes at once, a brand kit, caption translation with a phone voice to dub it, a hook coach for your first seconds, and smart B-roll found in your gallery on the phone.
- **Shared templates:** share a template as a link; whoever opens it gets the cuts, timing, texts and effects, and adds their own clips and music.
- **And more:** a short welcome that sets up the editor for you (skipped if you already have projects), Simple mode for a shorter toolbar, update notices with What's new, an optional "Made with PreCut" end card (off unless you turn it on), and Settings links to the privacy policy, terms and help.
