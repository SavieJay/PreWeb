## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one continuous section drawn through an edit: the timeline is the building, the playhead is the sunbeam, and the visitor's scroll carries it from 0:00 to the export. It refuses the dark glowing phone-on-gradient over a grid of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Limewash plaster ground #F2EFEA; charcoal poché #1C1F22 for section cuts, the phone body, the ground slab and the footer; one sunbeam gold #D6A43A for the playhead, the primary action and the active time; shadow blue #4E5C6B for past and inactive times (darkened from the board's #5B6B7C so it holds 6:1 as text); mortar #D9DCDD panels. Hairline rules, leader-line annotations ending in dots, chamfered buttons, readout rows split by vertical hairlines. Barlow Condensed inclined caps tracked wide for display and labels; tabular times with a gold colon; Roboto Flex upright for body.

STORY: The visitor sees the real editor playing inside a section drawing, reads "Pro-level video editing on your phone. No watermark.", follows the playhead through what each tool does, trusts the plain facts (size, version, what leaves the phone), and downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: a title block top left with the inclined headline, "No watermark." lit by a slanted gold beam, a one-line tagline, the gold chamfered "Download for Android" plus an outlined "See it work", and readouts (version, size, Android).
